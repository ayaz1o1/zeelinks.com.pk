Capabilities

What the AI actually watches for

Every rule is tuned per camera and per zone during commissioning, so you get alerts that matter and not a flood of false alarms.

Intrusion & perimeter

Line-crossing and zone rules that fire only for people or vehicles, with day and night schedules.

People counting & occupancy

Entry and exit counts, live occupancy and queue length, exported as daily or weekly reports.

ANPR & vehicle logs

Read number plates at gates, auto-open barriers for whitelisted vehicles, keep a searchable in/out record.

PPE & workplace safety

Missing helmet or vest detection, forklift–pedestrian proximity and restricted-area entry.

Loitering & unusual behaviour

People lingering near ATMs, cash counters or stock rooms, plus sudden crowd formation and running.

Heat maps & dwell time

See which aisles, counters or displays get attention — and which are dead space.

Smart video search

Search recordings by object and attribute — “white car, back gate, Tuesday night” — instead of scrolling timelines.

Face recognition & attendance

Optional staff recognition for attendance and door access, plus watchlist alerts where permitted.

Asset & shrinkage alerts

Object-removed and abandoned-object rules for stores, warehouses and server rooms.

Questions

Common questions before a deployment

Do I need new cameras?

Usually not. Most 2MP+ IP cameras with a reasonable angle and decent lighting work fine. We tell you honestly which few positions need upgrading.

Where is my video stored?

On your own NVR and analytics server by default. Cloud is optional if you want off-site backup or multi-branch dashboards.

Will I get false alarms?

Far fewer than motion detection, because rules only trigger on recognised people or vehicles. We re-tune after the first week of real traffic.

How much does it cost?

It depends on camera count and which analytics you need. After a free survey you get a fixed, itemised quotation — no open-ended charges.
