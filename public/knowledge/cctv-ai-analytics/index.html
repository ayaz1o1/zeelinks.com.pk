CCTV Smart Analytics: Motion, Human and Vehicle Detection

Understand basic motion detection and smarter CCTV analytics for practical surveillance.

Motion detection

Traditional motion detection reacts to image changes and can be affected by shadows, trees, rain and lighting.

Classification

Modern cameras may classify people or vehicles, but capabilities and accuracy vary by model, firmware and scene.

Perimeter analytics

Line crossing and intrusion regions can help protect entrances when camera position and target direction are suitable.

Test analytics

False positives and missed events are possible. Tune sensitivity, regions and schedules in the real environment.
