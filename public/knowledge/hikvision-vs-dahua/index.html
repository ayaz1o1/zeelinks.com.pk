Hikvision vs Dahua CCTV: A Practical System-Selection Framework

Compare Hikvision and Dahua CCTV systems by exact model capabilities, image quality, recorder limits, analytics and project requirements.

Do not compare brands by logo alone

Both manufacturers have broad product families. A meaningful comparison requires the exact camera and NVR models, firmware and intended application.

Camera comparison

Evaluate sensor, lens, low-light behavior, WDR, codec, bitrate range, FPS, illumination and analytics rather than megapixel count alone.

Recorder comparison

Check incoming bandwidth, decoding capability, supported camera resolutions, storage interfaces, alarm I/O and network architecture.

Ecosystem and compatibility

Consider app behavior, firmware management, third-party compatibility, switch architecture, support resources and the availability of replacement units.

Project fit wins

The best choice depends on the scene, budget, maintenance expectations, local availability and installer capability. Document why the selected model fits the actual requirement.
