Why Binary Matters in Subnetting

IPv4 addresses contain 32 bits, divided into four 8-bit octets. A CIDR prefix tells you how many of those bits are network bits.

Example: 192.168.10.25/24
The first 24 bits identify the network. The final 8 bits identify hosts within that subnet.
