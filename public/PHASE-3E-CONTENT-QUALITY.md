## Content direction
Future guides should follow the same structure where useful:
1. What the technology/problem actually means
2. How to calculate or diagnose it
3. What can go wrong
4. Practical field checklist
5. Exact-model/specification caveats
6. Related Zee Links calculator/tool
7. Related guides for the next decision

## Remaining thin pages after this pass
42 guide pages remain below approximately 350 words. These should be expanded in focused batches, prioritizing pages with the strongest search intent and closest relationship to the calculators.

## Principle
Do not mass-publish thin keyword pages. Each new guide should answer a real technician, installer, administrator or buyer question with enough practical detail to be useful without leaving the site.
